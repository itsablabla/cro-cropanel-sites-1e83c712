Voice of customer

What customers actually say

Gathered from third-party reviews, forums and video — the site’s own pages are excluded. Every quote was verified word-for-word against the page it links to; anything that could not be verified was discarded rather than paraphrased.

Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Hidden fees revealed late
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Prospects are deterred when unexpected costs appear at checkout, increasing cart abandonment and distrust.
Drip pricing confuses total cost
Sometimes vendors create slowly accumulating costs as you progress through check out.
— shawlewenz.com
Gradual addition of fees during checkout confuses customers about the final price, leading to frustration and potential purchase abandonment.
Junk fees inflate final price
Junk fees are unexpected, hidden, or mandatory charges added to the advertised price of a product or service, often revealed late in the checkout process or after the purchase.
— alliantcreditunion.org
Unexpected mandatory fees make it hard to compare prices and can significantly increase the actual cost, causing buyer's remorse.
Unclear refund policy harms trust
If your refund policy is unclear, it can harm customer experience and make your roadmap for processing requests difficult.
— squarespace.com
Ambiguous return or refund terms create uncertainty and risk for prospects, potentially deterring them from completing a purchase.

Said after buying — these shape what the page must reassure

Poor service after payment
Asma is extremely rude. She seems to not care about your travel plans once you pay her.
— embedsocial.com
Customers feel neglected once they've paid, leading to negative reviews and churn.
Unacknowledged delays frustrate customers
Waited 20 minutes after my appointment time and no one acknowledged the delay. Not great for a first visit.
— appfollow.io
When service delays are not communicated, customers feel disrespected and may not return.
